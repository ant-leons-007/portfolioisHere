About

I've spent the last seven years moving between product design and front-end development, usually landing wherever a team needs someone to carry a feature from a rough idea to something people can actually use. I care most about the small decisions — copy, spacing, the way a loading state feels — that add up to whether a product feels considered or just finished.

Outside of client work, I keep a running list of small tools worth building for their own sake, and usually finish one or two a year.
